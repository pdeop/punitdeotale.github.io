I opened my TV the other day and it needed an update before it would let me watch anything. My car's infotainment rebooted at a red light. A banking app I use logged me out mid-transfer, twice. None of this is catastrophic. That's exactly the point — we've quietly accepted a baseline of broken that would have been unthinkable a decade ago.

Everything is buggy now. And I don't think it's an accident.
